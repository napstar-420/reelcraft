title: Channels

A **channel** is the home for one kind of video you make, such as a YouTube channel or a series of
reels. Everything you build for it lives inside it: its blueprints, characters, assets and runs.
Two channels never share any of these.

## Create a channel

1. Open **Channels** in the sidebar.
2. Select **New channel**.
3. Fill in the form:
   - **Name**: required.
   - **Description**: optional, up to 255 characters. The counter under the box shows how many
     you've used.
   - **Themes**: optional labels such as `Comedy, Motivational`. Separate them with commas. They're
     shown on the channel's card to help you tell channels apart. Reelcraft doesn't use them for
     anything else.
   - **Defaults**: optional settings every blueprint in the channel starts from, such as a default
     model. See [Defaults](./defaults.md).
4. Select **Create**.

If the channel can't be saved, the reason is shown above the **Create** button and the form stays
open, so you can fix it and try again.

## The channel page

Select a channel's card to open its page. Its tabs show how many of each item it has:

| Tab            | What's there                                                                     |
| -------------- | -------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Blueprints** | The recipes for your videos. See [Blueprints](../blueprints/blueprints.md).      |
| **Characters** | People or mascots that appear in your videos. See [Characters](./characters.md). |
| **Assets**     | Images, video and audio you upload to reuse. See [Assets](./assets.md).          |
| **Runs**       | Every run of this channel's blueprints. See [Runs list](../runs/runs-list.md).   |

![A channel's page, with the Blueprints, Characters, Assets and Runs tabs](/img/usage/channel-page.png)

## Edit a channel

On the channel page, open **More** and select **Edit**. You can also select the **⋯** button on
the channel's card in the Channels list. Change what you need, then select **Save changes**.

The **ⓘ** button on a card shows the channel's details without editing them.

## Archive a channel

Archiving hides a channel from the Channels list without changing anything else. Its blueprints,
runs and files stay as they are.

1. Open **More** on the channel page, or **⋯** on its card, and select **Archive**.
2. Confirm with **Archive**.

To see archived channels, turn on **Show archived channels** at the top of the Channels list.
Archived channels show an **Archived** badge. To bring one back, select **Unarchive** in the same
menu.

## Delete a channel

Deleting a channel permanently removes it and **everything in it**: every blueprint and version,
every run and its outputs, every character and every asset. It can't be undone.

1. Open **More** on the channel page, or **⋯** on its card, and select **Delete**.
2. Type the channel's name exactly as shown.
3. Select **Delete permanently**.

You return to the Channels list, with a message saying the channel was deleted.

:::note Runs in progress block a delete

You can't delete a channel while any of its runs is still going, including runs that are paused
or waiting for you. Finish or cancel those runs first. See
[Pause, resume and cancel](../runs/pause-resume-cancel.md).

:::

The files the channel's runs made, and the files you uploaded, are removed from storage in the
background. By default that happens about 30 days after the delete.
